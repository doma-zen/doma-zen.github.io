Program name and description

Program name: Doma Mantra messaging program. Doma Mantra answers, routes, books, and follows up for small businesses, by chat, voice, text, and phone. By text, we send appointment confirmations, reminders, and call notifications — only to people who asked to be contacted.

How you opt in

You opt in by giving your phone number on the website form and agreeing to receive texts, or by asking by phone to be contacted by text. We only send texts to people who asked for them.

Message frequency and rates

Message frequency varies with what you asked for — a confirmation, a reminder, or a call notification. Message and data rates may apply, as charged by your carrier. No other charge for receiving these texts is made by Doma Mantra in this program.

How to opt out, and how to get help

Reply STOP to opt out at any time. Reply HELP for help. After you opt out, we will stop sending program texts, except for a single confirmation that you opted out.

Support contact

For help with this program, contact us through the website form on this site, or reply HELP to any program text. No name or phone number is published on this page.

What this service is, and is not

Doma Mantra helps businesses answer and follow up. It does not replace the business, and it does not make promises on a business's behalf beyond what that business taught it. Every conversation is kept on the record, so what was promised can be checked.
