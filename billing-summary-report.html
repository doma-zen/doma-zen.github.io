🤝 How this builds your Trust Index

Trust has two qualities: intentions (you mean it, and you keep it under pressure) and competence (you can actually do it). High in one and low in the other is low trust. The Index is built from commitments you make to your own customers — kept, verified, and scored over time.

  • Kept promises — verified in routing data, callbacks, and reviews. “We answer, on time, every time” — did you?
  • Complete cycles — every case heard, ruled, and recorded, good or bad. No case left hanging.
  • Validity behind every event — recording + transcript + verdict. No guess, no unproven bill.
  • Both sides counted — the customer's pattern counts too: first contact, repeat contact, and abuse flags, all on the record.

Sample Trust Index movement this month: ↑ up — sample direction only, no score is claimed. A real score needs real data, in Postgres, over time.
