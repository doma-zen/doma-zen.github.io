This is a working sample of the public face, and the face is Franklin. Type a question, or press the microphone and talk. Franklin answers out loud from the business's own material, asks a question back, and shows his work as he goes.

How the Mantra way works

Three steps, in plain words.

1

Ask the right questions

Mantra starts by asking the owner about the business, its customers, and the promises it makes.

2

Learn the business

It learns from the owner's answers and the business's own documents, and it remembers them.

3

Count the promises

Every promise is put on the record and counted, so nothing is left to memory.
