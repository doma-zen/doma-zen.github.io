Sample — the front door, staffed

Are you open today? And where's the market stall?
Greeter: Both! The shop on Damen is open 9 to 5 today, and this morning we're at Green City Market in Lincoln Park until about 1. Whichever's closer — the full case is at the shop.
What's actually in the chicken tenders? My dog has allergies.
Greeter: One ingredient: chicken breast from Slagel Family Farm, right here in Illinois — hand-sliced and dehydrated, nothing added, ever. 90% protein on the guaranteed analysis. For allergy pups, tell me what you're avoiding and I'll match the treat to it.
I saw on Facebook the salmon's back?
Greeter: It is — freeze-dried salmon, back in stock this week, $13. It goes fast. Want me to set a bag aside at Damen under your name?
Can Franklin get a treat packet every two weeks without me remembering to reorder?
Greeter: Yes. Tell me Franklin's favorites and anything he can't have, and I'll build his packet, remind you before each one, and have it ready every two weeks for pickup or shipping. Pause it or swap a treat any time.
Can I pay for Franklin's packets with a gift card I load up front?
Greeter: You can. Load the card once, and I'll draw each packet from the balance, show you what's left, and warn you before it runs low. If Kat is offering a load-up bonus, I'll apply exactly the terms she approved — no surprise charges.
It's my sister's dog's birthday next week.
Greeter: A BARKday, then. We do gift cards — I can put $25 on one with the doggie doughnut on the front and a note from you, and email it to her today. Whose name goes on the card — the dog's, obviously?
I run a grooming salon — do you wholesale?
Greeter: We do — shops and cafés across the city carry us. Three quick things so I can hand Kat a clean brief: your salon's name, roughly how much you'd start with, and where you're located. She'll write you back herself.
