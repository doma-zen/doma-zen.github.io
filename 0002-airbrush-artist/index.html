How a piece happens.

No forms that feel like homework. You talk, he listens, and you see the idea before it's painted.

1 · Send the spark

A photo, a screenshot, a team logo — or just words. "Something like this."

2 · Say it your way

What it goes on, how big, which colors, what names. Type it or talk it — in English or Spanish.

3 · See it first

He sends back a concept — a prototype of your piece. Change anything. Nothing gets painted until it looks right to you.

4 · He paints it

By hand, layer by layer, in colors that don't fade into the crowd. Pick it up or catch him live at your event.

Making it easy — and giving back.

Ways to grow the brand that cost him little and mean a lot in the neighborhood. He picks which ones are his — nothing here is assumed.

  • GIFT CARDS · Grandma buys the card; the kid designs the piece. Money in his hand before paint ever dries — and the person who redeems it walks in already happy.
  • EASY PAY · Cash App and the ways his customers already pay. No card machine standing between a yes and a deposit.
  • BRING YOUR OWN · Bring your own shirt or hat and the price drops — he supplies the art, you supply the cloth. Shops already do it; customers love it.
  • GIVE BACK · A donated piece for the school raffle. A free cap for the team's hardest worker. His call, his neighborhood, his name on it — generosity is the loudest advertising there is.
  • LIVE AT YOUR PARTY · Book the booth: birthdays, quinceañeras, game days. Guests leave wearing the souvenir, and every photo they post carries his work.
  • YOU BRING IT, WE HAND IT BACK · You give us the photo, the logo, the rough idea — you get back clean icons, images, and art ready to paint. His creativity, amplified.
