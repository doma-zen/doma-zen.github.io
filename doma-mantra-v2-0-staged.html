Same pattern in any trade: what it is, what condition it is in, pickup or delivery, and price or trade. Both cards are example only.

RESTAURANT EQUIPMENT · EXAMPLE ONLY

The fryer

Have an item to sell. A restaurant has a commercial fryer to sell after a kitchen refit. Condition: used, cleaned, working, sold as seen. Pickup or delivery: pickup preferred, local delivery can be arranged. Price or trade: price, or trade for shelving the kitchen needs.

Want an item to get. Another kitchen wants to get a fryer, because theirs failed before a busy weekend. Condition wanted: working, any age, must heat evenly. Pickup or delivery: can pick up same day. Price or trade: price, or trade catering help for an event.

The Want and the Have match. That match is the point.

AUTOMOTIVE · EXAMPLE ONLY

The part, and the pump

Want an item to get. An expert working with an automotive company wants a specific part, a pump, for a job that is waiting. Condition wanted: new, or tested used that is known good. Pickup or delivery: delivery to the shop, or pickup within the area. Price or trade: price, or trade diagnostic time.

Have an item to sell, give, or trade. The same expert has a surplus part, a pump, he is trying to get rid of. Condition: surplus, bench-tested, working. Pickup or delivery: pickup from the shop, shipping can be arranged. Price or trade: sell, give to a shop that needs it, or trade for a part he does need.

Items cover restaurant equipment, auto parts, and more, not just one trade.
