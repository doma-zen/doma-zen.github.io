Your chauffeur answers

Every call, text, and chat answered at one front door, booked and followed up.

  • Greets, triages, routes, books. What is it, how urgent, where? Then to the right person, with the context carried over.
  • Warm handoff, never a dead end. When it can’t answer, a person takes over with the full story, so the caller never repeats themselves.
  • Booked means told, four ways. Customer and business each get a text and an email. No party, no channel skipped.
  • We call it a chauffeur, not an attendant. A chauffeur knows the route and opens the door.

Three voices check every answer

The same question runs in three separate lanes. Where the lanes disagree is where the learning is.

OWNER

What should happen. Your rules, your promises, your way.

QA

Where it breaks. The hard questions, the loaded questions, the edge cases, tested before a customer finds them.

OPERATOR

What actually happens. The person on the floor, in the truck, at the counter. We ask the operator, not the ivory tower.

    Followed up. A reminder the day before, and after the visit, a thank-you with a gift card she can hand to her neighbour. That’s the whole system, working quietly.
